Andar Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Andar Studio creates games, operating systems, Android applications, and websites. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when users use our products and services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the product, Andar Studio may collect limited information such as:

  • Device information (device model and operating system version)
  • Application performance information
  • Crash reports and error information
  • Settings and preferences saved by applications
  • Information provided when contacting us

We do not collect personal files, photos, documents, or private content unless a feature requires permission.

2. How We Use Information

  • Improve our apps, games, and services
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Improve performance
  • Maintain security

3. Data Storage

Some applications may store data locally on your device, such as game progress, settings, and preferences.

4. Third-Party Services

Some Andar Studio products may use third-party services such as app stores, analytics, or advertising services. These services may have their own privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

Andar Studio does not sell personal information. Information may only be shared when required by law or needed to provide services.

6. Children's Privacy

Andar Studio respects user privacy and aims to minimize unnecessary data collection.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no digital service can guarantee complete security.

8. Changes to This Policy

Andar Studio may update this Privacy Policy when our products and services change.
